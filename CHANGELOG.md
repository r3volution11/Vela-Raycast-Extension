# Vela Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- **Search Conversations**: every channel and private conversation in Vela, with mentions first, then unread, then the rest in sidebar order. Open one in Vela, catch up on it, open its Recap, write or draft a message, mark it read, mark everything read, or copy its `vela://` link.
- **Send Message**: pick a channel you're in or a private conversation and send a message as plain text. More than 3 lines asks first.
- **Catch Me Up**: open Catch me up in Vela for the unmuted conversation with the most unread messages.
- **Unread Conversations**: an optional menu bar command (off by default) with your mentions and the conversations that have something unread, refreshed every 5 minutes.
- An **App** preference to use Vela Dev, Vela's development build, instead of Vela.
